# DM4 · Welcome: Parent Ambassador access + codes

**Use case:** They said yes. This welcomes them to the Parent Ambassador community and gives them their free access code and their referral code.

**Before sending:** In the affiliate's drawer (**Their link and results**), fill in **Access code** (their family's free access) and **Referral code** (the one they share). The DM fills both in. Add **Their tracking link** too if they have one, so results can be tracked per affiliate.

**When it's sent:** The affiliate is **In conversation**. After logging, the next step is to check they've signed up and posted (5 business days). When they post, click **Posted / shared link** with the post's link in the note: that moves them to **Sharing our link**.

## Subject

_None (DM)._

## Body

```text
Hi {{greeting}}! We'd love to welcome you to the CyberSafely Parent Ambassador community! 💙

Here's what we'd love to offer you:
✨ Free CyberSafely access for your family, so you can explore the platform and see how it works firsthand.
🔑 Your personal access code, so you can get started without paying: {{access_code}}
🔗 Your referral code to share with followers who may be interested in protecting their children's digital wellbeing: {{code}}
📱 Easy setup and support. We'll walk you through getting started and we're here if you have any questions.

Our goal is to connect with parents who care about helping families navigate the digital world safely. We'd love for you to experience CyberSafely yourself and, if you genuinely find it valuable, share it with your community.

Ready to get started? You can sign up here: https://cybersafely.ai/login

Once you've signed up, let me know if you have any questions or need help getting your family set up. We're excited about the possibility of working together!

{{sender}}, The CyberSafely Team
```

## Why it's written this way

- **The codes are in the message.** The access code and referral code are filled in from the drawer, so they don't need a second message to get started.
- **"If you genuinely find it valuable."** Keeps the ask honest: try first, share only if they like it.
- **Signed by a person.** Your name keeps the thread personal; "The CyberSafely Team" shows it's official.
