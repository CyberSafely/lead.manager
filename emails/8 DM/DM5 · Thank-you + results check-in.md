# DM5 · Thank-you + results check-in

**Use case:** After the ambassador posts, and then monthly while they're active: thank them and share how their code is doing.

**Before sending:** Update **Sign-ups from their link** in the drawer so `{{signups}}` is current. If there are none yet, drop that sentence.

**When it's sent:** The affiliate is **Sharing our link** or **Driving sign-ups**. No stage change.

## Subject

_None (DM)._

## Body

```text
Hi {{greeting}}! Thank you so much for sharing CyberSafely with your community 💙 {{signups}} families have signed up with your code so far!

How is it going for your family? Anything you need from us for your next post?
```

## Why it's written this way

- **Real numbers.** Showing results keeps ambassadors posting.
- **Asks about their family first.** They're users too, and their experience is what they share.
- **Honest.** Only send a number you've measured (the drawer says "not yet measured" until you enter one).
