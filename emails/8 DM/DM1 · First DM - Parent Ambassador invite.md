# DM1 · First DM: Parent Ambassador invite

**Use case:** The first message to a parent creator on the **Affiliates** list. It invites them to try CyberSafely free with their own family and, if it fits, join the Parent Ambassador community.

**How to send:** Open the affiliate in the Lead Manager (Affiliates → click them), **Copy DM**, **Open profile to DM**, and paste it into a message. Then **Log DM sent**.

**Personalize it:** swap the "I came across your content…" line for something specific from one of their recent posts when you can. It's the line that gets replies.

**When it's sent:** The affiliate is **Not contacted**. After logging it moves to **DM sent**, and the follow-up (DM2) is due in 3 business days.

## Subject

_None (DM)._

## Body

```text
Hi {{greeting}}! 💙

I'm {{sender}}, reaching out from CyberSafely.ai, a digital safety platform designed to help parents recognize concerning online behavior and better support their kids' digital wellbeing.

I came across your content and love the conversations you share about parenting and family life. I thought you might connect with what we're doing!

We're inviting a small group of parent creators to try CyberSafely with their own families at no cost, and potentially become part of our Parent Ambassador community. If it's a good fit for your family, we'd also give you a personalized code to share with your audience.

There's no obligation to recommend anything you haven't personally tried. We're simply looking to connect with parents who care about helping families navigate the digital world.

Would you be interested in learning more? 📲
```

## Why it's written this way

- **Try it first, recommend later.** Leading with free access for their own family, and "no obligation to recommend anything you haven't personally tried", respects their credibility with their audience.
- **A small group.** Makes the invite feel selected, not mass-sent.
- **From a person.** Your first name makes it read like a person, not a brand account.
- **One easy question to close.** "Interested in learning more?" is easy to answer in a DM.

## Open before sending

- Confirm the platform description ("help parents recognize concerning online behavior…") is approved wording (see the claims items in Email Flow).
- Don't send from a brand-new or empty account: those DMs land in message requests or get filtered.
