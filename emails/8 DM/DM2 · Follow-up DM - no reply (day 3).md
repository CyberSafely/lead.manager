# DM2 · Follow-up DM: no reply (day 3)

**Use case:** One short bump when DM1 got no reply after 3 business days.

**How to send:** Reply in the same DM thread so they see the first message. **Log DM sent** afterwards.

**When it's sent:** The affiliate is **DM sent**. If they still haven't answered 5 business days later, they move to **No reply**. Don't send a third DM.

## Subject

_None (DM)._

## Body

```text
Hi {{greeting}}! Just floating this back up in case it got lost in your message requests 💙 We'd still love to give your family free access to CyberSafely. No obligation at all, and if now isn't a good time, totally understood!
```

## Why it's written this way

- **Short and in-thread.** Keeps the first message visible without repeating it.
- **Restates the offer in one line.** Free access for their family is the reason to reply.
- **Easy out.** Makes a "no" as easy as a "yes", which gets more replies either way.
