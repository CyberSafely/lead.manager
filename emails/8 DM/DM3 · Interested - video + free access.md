# DM3 · Interested: video + free access

**Use case:** They replied that they're interested. This shares the short explainer video and repeats the free-access offer.

**Before sending:** Click **Replied** (or **Agreed to partner**) in the affiliate's drawer first. That moves them to **In conversation**, and DM3 becomes the suggested DM.

**When it's sent:** The affiliate is **In conversation**. After logging, the next step is the welcome message (DM4) once they say yes.

## Subject

_None (DM)._

## Body

```text
Amazing! I'd love to share a little more about what we're building. We have a short video that explains how CyberSafely uses AI to help parents recognize concerning online behavior while respecting their child's privacy:
https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dc_f1R2C8pq/

We'd love to give you free access to try it with your family and see if it's something you'd feel comfortable sharing with your community. Want me to send over your access details?
```

## Why it's written this way

- **Show, don't explain.** The video does the explaining, so the DM stays short.
- **Ends with a yes/no question.** "Want me to send over your access details?" makes the next step easy, and their "yes" is the cue for DM4.
- **Clean video link.** The `?vrfl=…` part of the original link is Instagram's share-tracking tag, which identifies who shared it. It's removed here; the link opens the same reel.

## Open before sending

- Confirm the AI / privacy wording is approved (see the claims items in Email Flow).
